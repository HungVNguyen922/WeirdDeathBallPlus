Who plays

Or press 1 / 2 (3 / 4 in 2v2). Set both to AI to watch them play.

Match

Flex teams

Each player can be Blue, Red or Off, Human or AI. With a team empty it is a sandbox: no score, and a player who dies comes back after 2 s. Changing anything restarts the match.

Special ability

Offline: one row per player you control (computer players always use Dash). Online: your own seat.

Visuals

Hides the Hyper, Xeric and Quake graphics (and the quake screen shake). The mechanics are unchanged.

Sound

Chat

Press Enter to write a message. Online it goes to everyone in the room; offline it is just a notepad.

Online

Connection status shows on the floor, under the score.

Click the game once so it gets keyboard focus.

Touch

  • Left half: touch and drag to move. Where you first touch becomes the centre; the direction you drag picks one of 8 arrow-key directions.
  • Snap the aim: the centre stays put for a moment after you lift, so tap the same direction twice quickly to snap the arrow's aim.
  • Right half: top third = special, middle = weight, bottom = grapple. Touching the line between two thirds presses both.

Shortcuts

  • Esc open / close this menu (it also closes the special-ability list first)
  • R restart the match
  • T switch between 1v1 and 2v2
  • 1 2 3 4 toggle AI for Blue, Red, Blue 2, Red 2 (3 and 4 only exist in 2v2)
  • M music on / off
  • Enter write a chat message (Enter sends it, Esc cancels). Online it goes to the whole room; offline it is a notepad
  • On a pick screen: arrows move, grapple or special locks in, weight takes it back

Tips

  • Click the special keycap beside Blue's or Red's goal to pick a different special ability.
  • Online, both the Blue and the Red layout above control your seat.
